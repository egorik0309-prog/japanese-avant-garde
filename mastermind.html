Mastermind
Japan

Where Japanese craftsmanship meets rebellious streetwear, luxury materials and a powerful visual identity.

TOKYO / JAPAN

LUXURY WITH A REBELLIOUS SPIRIT

A single symbol became the foundation of an entire fashion identity.

Founded by Masaaki Homma in 1997, mastermind JAPAN developed a distinctive approach to luxury streetwear.

Its designs combine premium materials, Japanese craftsmanship and a darker visual aesthetic. The contrast between refined construction and rebellious graphics became an important part of the brand's identity.

The skull and crossbones symbol is perhaps its most recognisable feature. It appears across clothing and accessories, transforming a simple graphic into a powerful visual signature.

Behind the bold appearance is a strong interest in quality, detail and the techniques used to create each garment.

Early Life

Masaaki Homma is the Japanese designer and founder of mastermind JAPAN. His approach to fashion developed through personal experimentation, observation and a deep interest in clothing.

Homma has described his design process as strongly connected to everyday life. He pays attention to details such as colour combinations, proportions and the way people wear their clothes.

Rather than relying on computer-generated designs, he has explained that he prefers drawing his ideas by hand.

In 1997, he established mastermind JAPAN. The brand introduced its first menswear collection in 1998.

Key Collections

In its early years, mastermind JAPAN focused on developing high-quality everyday clothing using Japanese materials and careful construction.

In 2001, the brand began presenting its work at international trade exhibitions in Paris. From 2003 to 2011, it regularly presented collections there.

Its skull and crossbones motif gradually became the central feature of the brand. Homma experimented with numerous hand-drawn versions before developing the final symbol.

In 2013, after celebrating its fifteenth anniversary, mastermind JAPAN temporarily suspended its regular collection activities.

The brand returned in Autumn/Winter 2017, introducing a new chapter that included both mastermind JAPAN and MASTERMIND WORLD.

Philosophy

The philosophy of mastermind JAPAN centres on the combination of luxury, functionality and individuality.

The brand places great importance on Japanese materials, traditional techniques and modern technology. These elements are brought together to create clothing with a strong focus on quality and construction.

Its famous skull and crossbones emblem represents the rebellious side of the brand. At the same time, the carefully selected fabrics and precise details create a contrast between streetwear and luxury fashion.

The brand also promotes the idea of "LOVE & PEACE", showing that its darker visual identity can exist alongside a positive philosophy.

Influence

mastermind JAPAN became an important name in Japanese luxury streetwear. Its approach helped bring together elements of high-end fashion and everyday urban clothing.

The brand also became known for collaborations with international companies and designers, allowing its distinctive visual identity to reach different audiences.

In 2017, the introduction of MASTERMIND WORLD expanded the brand's international presence while continuing its original design philosophy.

Today, mastermind JAPAN remains recognisable for its skull emblem, dark aesthetic and commitment to Japanese craftsmanship.

Undercover

A world where punk culture, art, imagination and fashion meet in unexpected ways.

TOKYO / PARIS

BEYOND THE EXPECTED

What happens when fashion becomes a meeting point between reality and imagination?

Founded by Jun Takahashi, Undercover developed a distinctive approach to fashion by combining punk culture, art, music and unusual visual references.

Its collections often bring together contrasting ideas: delicate and rough materials, familiar clothing and unexpected shapes, beauty and decay.

Rather than simply following fashion trends, Takahashi creates collections that tell stories and explore emotions.

This imaginative approach has made Undercover one of the most recognisable names in Japanese avant-garde fashion.

Early Life

Jun Takahashi was born in 1969 in Kiryu, Gunma Prefecture, Japan. His interest in fashion and music developed during his teenage years.

In 1988, he entered Bunka Fashion College in Tokyo, where he studied fashion design. During this period, he became increasingly interested in punk music and alternative culture.

In 1990, while still a student, Takahashi started Undercover with a friend. He graduated from Bunka Fashion College in 1991.

In 1993, he also opened the Nowhere boutique in Harajuku with Nigo. The shop became an important meeting point for Tokyo's emerging fashion and streetwear scene.

His first runway collection was presented in Tokyo in 1994.

Key Collections

One of Undercover's most significant moments came with the Spring/Summer 2003 collection called SCAB, presented during the brand's first Paris Fashion Week appearance.

Inspired by punk culture and ideas of destruction and reconstruction, the collection featured distressed fabrics, exposed stitching, unusual layers and reconstructed garments.

Another important collection was But Beautiful for Autumn/Winter 2004. It explored the relationship between delicate beauty and unusual, sometimes unsettling forms.

In Spring/Summer 2018, Takahashi presented a collection influenced by the mysterious atmosphere of the film The Shining.

His Spring/Summer 2024 collection continued his interest in fantasy and imagination through sculptural garments and nature-inspired details.

Philosophy

Undercover's philosophy is based on the freedom to experiment and challenge familiar ideas of fashion.

Punk music, films, literature, art and everyday experiences all influence Takahashi's creative process. He often translates visual references into unusual clothing and silhouettes.

One of the most interesting features of his work is the contrast between beauty and imperfection. Carefully constructed garments may contain distressed details, unusual shapes or unexpected materials.

Takahashi is also interested in the emotions hidden beneath the surface of everyday life. His collections often communicate feelings that cannot easily be expressed through words.

Influence

Undercover helped change the relationship between Japanese streetwear and international high fashion.

In 1997, Jun Takahashi received the Mainichi Fashion Grand Prix Newcomer Award. He received the main prize in 2001 and again in 2013.

The brand made its first appearance at Paris Fashion Week in 2002, bringing Takahashi's experimental approach to a wider international audience.

In 2015, a retrospective exhibition titled Labyrinth of Undercover: 25 Year Retrospective explored the designer's creative work.

Today, Undercover remains influential for its combination of fashion, music, art and storytelling. Its collections continue to show that clothing can be more than something we simply wear.

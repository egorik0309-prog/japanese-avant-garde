Number
(N)ine

A fashion language shaped by alternative music, youth culture and the emotional character of everyday clothing.

TOKYO / PARIS

FASHION MEETS MUSIC

What happens when the energy of rock music becomes part of the way we dress?

Number (N)ine explored this question through clothing that brought together music, personal expression and experimental design.

Created by Takahiro Miyashita, the label developed a visual identity influenced by alternative rock, vintage clothing and the atmosphere of youth culture.

Distressed fabrics, layered silhouettes, graphic details and carefully reconstructed garments gave familiar pieces an entirely different character.

Rather than treating fashion as something separate from everyday life, Miyashita connected it to the music and culture that shaped his own experiences.

Early Life

Takahiro Miyashita was born in Tokyo, Japan, in 1973. His early interest in clothing developed outside the traditional fashion education system.

During the 1990s, he worked at Nepenthes, a clothing store in Tokyo. This experience allowed him to study different garments, materials and styles directly.

Miyashita developed his knowledge through observation, experimentation and working with clothing rather than attending a traditional fashion school.

In November 1996, he established KOOKS Co., Ltd., launching Number (N)ine. The brand's first shop opened in Tokyo in 1997.

Key Collections

Number (N)ine became recognised for collections that used music and cultural references as starting points for design.

One of its important early collections was Nowhere Man, presented for Autumn/Winter 2002–03. It explored darker silhouettes and experimental menswear.

The label's Autumn/Winter 2003 collection Touch Me I'm Sick explored grunge-inspired styling, distressed fabrics and layered clothing.

In Autumn/Winter 2004, Miyashita presented Give Peace a Chance, the collection that marked Number (N)ine's Paris debut.

Other memorable collections included Night Crawlers in 2005 and Noir in 2006, each bringing different musical and visual influences into contemporary menswear.

Philosophy

Music was one of the strongest influences behind Miyashita's approach to fashion. His interests included alternative rock, punk and artists such as Kurt Cobain and David Bowie.

Even the name Number (N)ine was inspired by The Beatles' experimental track Revolution 9.

Instead of following conventional ideas of menswear, Miyashita experimented with unusual proportions, distressed materials, vintage references and unexpected layers.

His work often combined apparently contrasting elements: formal tailoring with casual garments, delicate details with rough textures, and familiar clothing with unusual construction.

Influence

Number (N)ine became an important part of the Japanese fashion movement of the late 1990s and 2000s. Its approach connected international youth culture with a distinctly Japanese design perspective.

Miyashita's original designer-led period ended after the Autumn/Winter 2009 collection, A Closed Feeling. In 2010, he started a new label called TAKAHIROMIYASHITATheSoloist.

The Number (N)ine name continued beyond his departure, without his original creative involvement.

In March 2026, Miyashita began a new independent chapter for Number (N)ine, bringing the name back under his own creative direction.

His work remains an example of how fashion can express music, memories, individuality and personal identity.
